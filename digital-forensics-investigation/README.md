# Digital Forensics & Expert Witness Investigation

## Overview

This project involved conducting a digital forensic investigation of
a provided disk image using E3 Forensic Platform.

The investigation focused on identifying and correlating digital
artifacts, reconstructing user activity, developing evidence-supported
findings, and communicating the results through a structured forensic
report.

## Objectives

- Examine a provided forensic disk image
- Identify relevant digital artifacts
- Analyze browser and search activity
- Examine email and metadata evidence
- Reconstruct a chronological timeline of user activity
- Correlate evidence across multiple artifact sources
- Develop evidence-supported investigative findings
- Document findings using forensic and expert-witness reporting
  principles

## Investigation Process

### Evidence Examination

The provided disk image was examined using E3 Forensic Platform to
identify artifacts relevant to the investigation.

Evidence sources analyzed included:

- Browser history
- Search activity
- Email artifacts
- File and artifact metadata
- Other relevant digital evidence

## Timeline Reconstruction

Relevant artifacts were organized chronologically to reconstruct user
activity.

The forensic timeline was used to identify relationships between
events and establish the sequence of activity represented by the
available evidence.

## Evidence Correlation

Artifacts from multiple evidence sources were compared and correlated
rather than relying on a single artifact in isolation.

This process was used to support investigative conclusions with
multiple pieces of relevant digital evidence where available.

## Forensic Reporting

Investigation findings and supporting evidence were documented in a
structured digital forensic report.

The report was designed to clearly distinguish technical evidence,
analysis, and investigative findings.

Expert-witness reporting principles were applied to communicate
technical findings in a clear and evidence-based manner.
